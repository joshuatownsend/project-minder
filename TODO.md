Phase 1 (Freshness + Telemetry + Budget + MEMORY.md index awareness on `/memory`) is in active planning per the approved plan at `~/.claude/plans/i-recently-read-this-temporal-crane.md`; the entries below are the deferred follow-on phases, in priority order.*
